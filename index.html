Founderealm

Sovereign business operating system

A business should own its infrastructure the way it owns its ovens, cameras and tools.

For people who make things or do things and have never touched a server.

In alpha. Not released yet.

From one sentence.

Say what you make or do. Founderealm builds the business's database, the workspace you run it from, and its website, then puts the site on a server you control.

You own the whole stack.

Your site runs on a secured Ubuntu server that you own and operate from your own computer: a box at home, or space Founderealm will rent you for a small fee.

No commission.

No sale pays a commission. You take money your own way, straight from your own site.

Yours, on your computer.

The business's data stays on your own computer. Any number of ventures run from one copy. No step should ask you for a terminal, a key or a server address; one that does is not finished.

Founderealm Lens is out now.

The instrument we built to build Founderealm, on its own: your whole codebase as one SQL database for Claude Code and VS Code, in any language. In public beta.
